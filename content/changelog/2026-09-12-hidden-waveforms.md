The JD-08’s panel stops at waveform 108, but 43 more are stored inside it: strings, brass, grand pianos and guitars. Toolkit’s wave browser lists all 151, and you can choose any of them for a tone.

You can hear a waveform before you pick it. Search by name or number, and Toolkit plays a short preview of each one as you move through the list. A patch built on a hidden waveform saves to the JD-08 like any other and is still there after you turn the synth off and on.
