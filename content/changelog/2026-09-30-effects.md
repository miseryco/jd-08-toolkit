They said it couldn’t be done! The other JD-08 editors we compared don’t let you edit the first effects group. None lists a control for its distortion, phaser, spectrum or enhancer, and group B gets six levels and a reverb time.

Toolkit edits both groups in full: 24 controls in group A and 30 in group B, including delay sync and reverb type. You can bypass any of the seven effects and change the order they run in. [The comparison](../alternatives/#comparison) shows what each editor covers.
