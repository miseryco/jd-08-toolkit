Click **Part A** or **Part B** and Toolkit opens the sound that’s on your JD-08 right now. Move a fader on the JD-08 and the editor follows. Drag a control in Toolkit and you hear the change on the synth.

The All view lines up the four tones side by side, so you can compare their waveforms, tuning and filters at a glance. When the sound is right, save it to your library or to a slot on the JD-08.
