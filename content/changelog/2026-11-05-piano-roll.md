The Patterns tab draws the JD-08’s step sequencer as a piano roll. Both parts share one roll, up to 64 steps long, and each step holds up to eight notes. Click **Part A** or **Part B** to choose which one you’re editing.

Draw notes, drag an edge to change a note’s length, and set velocity in the lane underneath. You can add motion lanes for controller moves and set an arpeggio for each part. Patterns save in your library beside the patches they use.
