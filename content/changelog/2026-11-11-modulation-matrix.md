Advanced Engine shows each tone’s modulation routes in a grid. Put velocity, aftertouch or an LFO in a column, choose a destination down the side, then drag the depth where they meet.

The menus offer the sources and destinations that moved the sound during testing on a JD-08. For example, you can use velocity to open the filter on one tone while leaving the other three alone.
