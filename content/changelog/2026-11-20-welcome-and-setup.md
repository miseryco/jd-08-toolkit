Toolkit now greets you when it opens. It looks for your JD-08, with a light running around the same four-digit display the synth has, then tells you whether it found one and points out the tabs, your JD-08’s parts and your library. Click anything to get started.

A new JD-08 needs a one-time setup before Toolkit can edit its sounds. A guide now walks you through it in about two minutes, showing what the JD-08’s display should read at each step. Toolkit saves an exact backup of your synth first, so you can undo the setup at any time.
